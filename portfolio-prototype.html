01 — About

Who's building this.

I'm Rifat — a software developer who ended up in AI/ML because I kept automating the boring parts of my own job.

Most of my work sits at the intersection of three things: building interfaces and services people actually use, training models that make a real decision instead of a demo prediction, and wiring the pipelines that keep data moving without anyone babysitting it.

I care about systems that don't need me around to keep working — clean APIs, typed code, monitored pipelines, models that degrade gracefully instead of silently. That mindset carries through everything on this page.
